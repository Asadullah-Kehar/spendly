Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 29, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your username and password (stored as a secure hash). When you log expenses, we collect the details you enter — amount, category, date, and any description you provide. We do not collect payment information, location data, or any data beyond what you explicitly enter into the app.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate the service — displaying your expense history, generating summaries, and enabling you to manage your records. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to the core functionality of Spendly.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database on the server where Spendly is hosted. Passwords are hashed before storage and are never stored or transmitted in plain text. You may delete your account at any time, which will permanently remove all associated data.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts. This involves your browser making a request to Google's servers, which may log your IP address per Google's own privacy policy. No other third-party services receive your data.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact details provided on this site. We will respond to reasonable requests regarding your data in a timely manner.
